# Fixed Deposits (FD) and Recurring Deposits (RD)

Fixed Deposits and Recurring Deposits are traditional savings instruments offered by banks and post offices in India. They are among the safest investment options.

## Fixed Deposit (FD)

### Key Features
- Lump sum deposited for a fixed tenure (7 days to 10 years)
- Fixed interest rate for the chosen tenure
- Interest payout options: monthly, quarterly, annual, or cumulative (at maturity)
- Minimum deposit: ₹1,000 (varies by bank)
- No upper limit on deposit amount

### Types of FDs
- **Regular FD:** Standard fixed deposit
- **Tax-Saver FD:** 5-year lock-in, tax benefit under 80C, up to ₹1.5L
- **Senior Citizen FD:** Higher interest rates (typically 0.25-0.75% extra)
- **Corporate FD:** Higher rates but higher risk (no DICGC insurance)
- **Cumulative FD:** Interest reinvested and paid at maturity (higher effective returns)

### Interest Rates (Current)
- Bank FDs: 6.5-8.5% p.a. (vary by bank and tenure)
- Small Savings (Post Office): 7.1-7.5% p.a. (revised quarterly)
- Corporate FDs: 7.5-10% p.a. (higher risk)
- Senior Citizen: Additional 0.25-0.75% p.a.

### Safety
- Bank FDs up to ₹5L insured by DICGC
- Post Office FDs backed by government (sovereign guarantee)
- Credit rating available for corporate FDs

### Tax on FD Interest
- Interest fully taxable as per income slab
- TDS at 10% if interest exceeds ₹40,000 (₹50,000 for senior citizens) per year
- Submit Form 15G/15H if income below taxable limit to avoid TDS

### Premature Withdrawal
- Allowed but penalty of 0.5-1% on interest rate
- Some banks allow partial withdrawal of FDs
- Tax-saver FDs cannot be withdrawn before 5 years

## Recurring Deposit (RD)

### Key Features
- Fixed amount deposited every month
- Tenure: 6 months to 10 years
- Interest rate: Similar to FDs of equivalent tenure
- Minimum monthly deposit: ₹100-500 (varies by bank)
- Ideal for salaried individuals building a corpus

### Interest Calculation
- Compound interest calculated quarterly
- Effective yield slightly less than FD of same rate (due to monthly deposits)
- Interest rate remains fixed throughout tenure

### Premature Closure
- Allowed with penalty (typically 1% reduction in interest rate)
- Some banks allow skipping deposits with small penalty

## FD vs RD vs PPF
- **FD:** Best for lump sum, short-medium term, higher liquidity
- **RD:** Best for regular savings (1-5 years), building emergency fund
- **PPF:** Best for long-term (15 years), tax-free returns, retirement

## Tips
- Ladder FDs of different tenures for liquidity + higher rates
- Use cumulative FDs for better tax planning (interest taxed at maturity)
- Senior citizens should utilize higher rates at their bank
- Compare rates across banks before booking
