# Insurance Basics for Indian Investors

Insurance is a contract where the insurer provides financial protection against specified losses in exchange for premium payments. IRDAI regulates the insurance sector in India.

## Life Insurance

### Term Insurance (Recommended)
- Pure protection plan with no maturity benefit
- High coverage (sum assured) at low premium
- Best for income replacement and family protection
- Premium for ₹1Cr cover at age 30: ~₹10,000-15,000/year
- Tax-free death benefit under Section 10(10D)

### Whole Life Insurance
- Coverage until age 99-100
- Higher premium than term
- Maturity benefit included

### ULIPs (Unit Linked Insurance Plans)
- Combines investment with insurance
- Premium partly goes to insurance, partly to market-linked funds
- 5-year lock-in; returns depend on market
- Higher costs than term + mutual fund combination

### Endowment Plans
- Traditional savings + insurance
- Guaranteed returns (low, typically 4-6%)
- Bonus declared annually
- Not recommended for most investors due to low returns

### Key Rule: Buy Term, Invest the Rest
Term insurance is almost always better than ULIP or endowment for pure protection needs. The premium savings can be invested in mutual funds for better long-term returns.

## Health Insurance

### Types
- **Individual Health Insurance:** Covers one person
- **Family Floater:** Covers entire family under single sum insured
- **Senior Citizen Health Insurance:** Higher premiums, limited entry age
- **Critical Illness:** Lump sum on diagnosis of specified illnesses
- **Top-Up/Super Top-Up:** Additional cover above base policy

### Key Features to Check
- Sum insured (recommended: minimum ₹5L for individual, ₹10L for family)
- Co-payment clause (percentage you pay)
- Room rent limits
- Pre-existing disease waiting period (typically 2-4 years)
- Network hospitals (cashless facility)
- No-claim bonus (cumulative bonus for claim-free years)
- Daycare procedures covered

### Tax Benefit
- Section 80D: Premium deduction up to ₹25,000 (₹50,000 for senior citizens)

## Motor Insurance
- **Third Party:** Mandatory by law, covers damage to others
- **Comprehensive:** Covers own vehicle damage + third party
- **Zero Depreciation Cover:** Recommended for new cars
- **No Claim Bonus:** Up to 50% discount for claim-free years

## General Tips
- Never mix insurance and investment
- Disclose all pre-existing conditions honestly
- Compare policies before buying (use IRDAI's website)
- Review coverage every 3-5 years as life circumstances change
- Port health insurance if dissatisfied with current insurer
