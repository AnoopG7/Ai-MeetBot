# Income Tax Basics for Individuals (India)

Income Tax in India is governed by the Income Tax Act, 1961, administered by the Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT). The financial year runs from April 1 to March 31.

## Tax Regimes

### New Tax Regime (Default from FY 2024-25)
- 0 to ₹3L: Nil
- ₹3L to ₹6L: 5%
- ₹6L to ₹9L: 10%
- ₹9L to ₹12L: 15%
- ₹12L to ₹15L: 20%
- Above ₹15L: 30%

Rebate under Section 87A: No tax if income ≤ ₹7L (new regime) or ≤ ₹5L (old regime).

### Old Tax Regime
- 0 to ₹2.5L: Nil
- ₹2.5L to ₹5L: 5%
- ₹5L to ₹10L: 20%
- Above ₹10L: 30%

Allows deductions (80C, 80D, HRA, LTA, etc.)

## Popular Deductions

### Section 80C (Max ₹1.5L)
- PPF, EPF, VPF contributions
- ELSS mutual funds (3-year lock-in)
- Life insurance premiums
- Tuition fees for children
- Principal repayment on home loan
- NSC, 5-year FD, Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana
- Tax Saving FDs (5-year lock-in)

### Section 80D (Health Insurance)
- Self + family: up to ₹25,000 (₹50,000 for senior citizens)
- Parents: up to ₹25,000 (₹50,000 for senior citizens)
- Preventive health checkup: up to ₹5,000

### Other Key Deductions
- **80CCD(1B):** NPS additional ₹50,000
- **80E:** Interest on education loans (no upper limit)
- **80G:** Donations to approved charities
- **24(b):** Home loan interest up to ₹2L
- **HRA:** House rent allowance (actual or 50%/40% of salary)

## Important Concepts
- **TDS:** Tax Deducted at Source (employer, bank, tenant deducts tax)
- **Advance Tax:** Pay in installments if tax liability > ₹10,000
- **ITR Forms:** ITR-1 (salaried), ITR-2 (capital gains), ITR-3 (business), ITR-4 (presumptive)
- **Form 16:** TDS certificate from employer
- **AIS (Annual Information Statement):** Consolidated tax statement

## Key Due Dates
- ITR filing: July 31 (individuals), October 31 (audit)
- Advance tax: June 15, September 15, December 15, March 15
- TDS return: Quarterly (7th of month after quarter)
- Belated return: Can file by December 31 with penalty

## Recent Changes (FY 2025-26)
- New tax regime made default; old regime still available
- Standard deduction of ₹75,000 introduced in new regime from 2025-26
- Revised income slabs for new tax regime
- TDS/TCS rationalization for easier compliance
