# Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a method of investing a fixed amount regularly (monthly, quarterly) into mutual funds. It is the most popular way for retail investors to participate in equity markets in India.

## How SIP Works
- Fixed amount invested at regular intervals
- Units purchased at prevailing NAV (Net Asset Value)
- More units when markets are low, fewer when high (rupee cost averaging)
- Power of compounding works over long periods

## Types of SIP
- **Regular SIP:** Fixed amount every month
- **Top-Up SIP:** Increase investment amount periodically (e.g., 10% yearly)
- **Flexible SIP:** Vary amount based on seasonal cash flows
- **Perpetual SIP:** No end date, continues until stopped
- **Trigger SIP:** Invests based on market conditions (NAV level)

## Benefits
- **Rupee Cost Averaging:** Reduces impact of market volatility
- **Power of Compounding:** Small amounts grow significantly over 10-20 years
- **Discipline:** Automatic investing builds savings habit
- **Low Entry Barrier:** Start with as little as ₹500 per month
- **Flexibility:** Stop, pause, increase, or decrease anytime

## Returns Expectations (Historical)
- Large Cap Funds: 12-14% p.a. over 10+ years
- Mid Cap Funds: 14-18% p.a. over long term (higher volatility)
- Small Cap Funds: 15-20% p.a. (very high volatility)
- Debt Funds: 7-9% p.a. (lower risk)
- Hybrid Funds: 10-12% p.a. (balanced risk)

## SIP vs Lump Sum
- SIP: Better for volatile markets, regular income investors
- Lump Sum: Better in falling markets, for experienced investors
- STP (Systematic Transfer Plan): Move lump sum gradually into equity

## Tax Implications
- ELSS funds have 3-year lock-in, tax benefit under 80C
- Equity funds: 10% LTCG tax on gains above ₹1 lakh (holding > 1 year)
- Debt funds: Taxed as per income slab (holding < 3 years)
- No tax on redemption up to ₹1 lakh LTCG in equity funds

## SIP Myths Debunked
- SIP does not guarantee returns or eliminate market risk
- SIP is not only for small investors; HNIs also use SIPs
- Stopping SIP during market falls defeats rupee cost averaging
