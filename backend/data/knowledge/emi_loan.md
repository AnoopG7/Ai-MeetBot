# Loans, EMI, and Credit Score (India)

Understanding loans, EMI calculations, and credit scores is essential for sound financial planning in India.

## Types of Loans

### Home Loan
- Purpose: Purchase/construction of residential property
- Loan amount: Up to 80-90% of property value (LTV ratio)
- Tenure: Up to 30 years
- Interest rate: 8.5-10% p.a. (floating or fixed)
- Tax benefits: Interest up to ₹2L under Section 24(b), principal under 80C

### Personal Loan
- Purpose: Any personal need (medical, wedding, travel)
- Loan amount: Up to ₹25-30L (based on income)
- Tenure: 1-5 years
- Interest rate: 10-24% p.a. (higher risk = higher rate)
- No collateral required (unsecured)
- Processing fee: 1-3% of loan amount

### Auto Loan
- Purpose: Purchase of new or used vehicle
- Loan amount: Up to 80-100% of on-road price
- Tenure: 1-7 years
- Interest rate: 8-14% p.a.
- Vehicle serves as collateral

### Education Loan
- Purpose: Higher education in India or abroad
- Loan amount: Up to ₹1Cr+ for foreign education
- Tenure: Up to 15 years after moratorium
- Interest rate: 8-12% p.a.
- Moratorium: Course period + 1 year
- Tax benefit: Interest deductible under Section 80E (no upper limit)

### Gold Loan
- Purpose: Quick funds against gold jewelry
- Loan amount: Up to 75-90% of gold value
- Tenure: 3-12 months
- Interest rate: 7-18% p.a.
- Quick processing, minimal documentation

## EMI Calculation

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1)

Where:
- P = Principal loan amount
- r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate / 12)
- n = Number of monthly installments

### Key Factors Affecting EMI
- **Principal:** Higher amount = higher EMI
- **Interest Rate:** Higher rate = higher EMI
- **Tenure:** Longer tenure = lower EMI but more total interest

### 30% Rule of Thumb
Total EMIs should not exceed 30-40% of monthly income for healthy finances.

## Credit Score (CIBIL)

### What is CIBIL Score
- A 3-digit number (300-900) summarizing creditworthiness
- Higher score = easier loan approval and better rates
- Maintained by TransUnion CIBIL (one of 4 credit bureaus in India)

### Score Range
- 750-900: Excellent (high approval chances)
- 650-749: Good (may face some scrutiny)
- 550-649: Fair (higher interest rates)
- Below 550: Poor (difficult to get loans)

### Factors Affecting CIBIL Score
- Payment history (35%): Always pay EMIs and credit card bills on time
- Credit utilization (30%): Use less than 30% of available credit limit
- Credit mix (10%): Healthy mix of secured (home, auto) and unsecured loans
- Credit age (15%): Longer credit history is better
- New inquiries (10%): Too many loan applications hurt score

### How to Improve CIBIL Score
- Pay all bills and EMIs before due date
- Keep credit card utilization below 30%
- Avoid multiple loan applications simultaneously
- Check credit report annually for errors
- Maintain old credit accounts (don't close them)
- Use secured credit card if starting fresh

## DTI (Debt-to-Income Ratio)
- Lenders prefer DTI below 40-50%
- DTI = Total monthly debt payments / Gross monthly income
- Higher DTI indicates over-leverage and higher default risk
