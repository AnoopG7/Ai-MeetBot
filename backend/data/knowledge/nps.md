# National Pension System (NPS)

The National Pension System (NPS) is a voluntary, defined-contribution retirement savings scheme regulated by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA). It was launched for government employees in 2004 and opened to all Indian citizens in 2009.

## Key Features
- **Account Types:** Tier I (pension account, restricted withdrawals) and Tier II (voluntary savings, no withdrawal restrictions)
- **Minimum Contribution (Tier I):** ₹500 per contribution, ₹6,000 per year
- **Minimum Contribution (Tier II):** ₹250 per contribution, no yearly minimum
- **Retirement Age:** 60 years (withdrawal); can defer up to 75
- **Tax Status:** EET (Exempt-Exempt-Taxed) except for Tier I

## Investment Options
- **Active Choice:** Investor chooses allocation across asset classes E (equity up to 75%), C (corporate bonds), G (government securities), A (alternative assets up to 5%)
- **Auto Choice (Lifecycle Fund):** Age-based automatic rebalancing
- **Fund Managers:** 8 registered Pension Fund Managers (SBI, UTI, HDFC, ICICI, Kotak, Aditya Birla, Tata, Max Life)

## Tax Benefits
- Employee contribution up to 10% of salary (basic + DA) deductible under 80CCD(1) within ₹1.5L 80C limit
- Additional deduction up to ₹50,000 under 80CCD(1B) over and above 80C limit
- Employer contribution up to 14% (central govt) or 10% (others) deductible under 80CCD(2) over 80C limit
- Tier II has no tax benefits

## Withdrawal Rules
- At 60: up to 60% tax-free lump sum, 40% must buy annuity
- Before 60: up to 25% withdrawal for specified purposes (higher education, marriage, home purchase, medical treatment)
- Premature exit (before 60): 80% must buy annuity if corpus > ₹2.5L, else full lump sum
- Partial withdrawal (3 times in entire tenure): up to 25% of own contributions

## NPS vs PPF vs EPF
- NPS: Market-linked returns, highest potential returns, 40% annuity lock-in
- PPF: Fixed returns, 15-year lock-in, sovereign guarantee, tax-free
- EPF: Fixed returns, employer-linked, tax-free up to interest threshold

## How to Open
- Online through e-NPS portal using Aadhaar or PAN
- Through Point of Presence (POP) like banks and post offices
- Tier II can be opened only if you have Tier I

## Recent Changes
- NPS Vatsalya: NPS for minors introduced in 2024
- Tax treatment for high-corpus subscribers changed in 2024-25 budget
- PFRDA allowed partial withdrawal up to 25% for specific purposes
