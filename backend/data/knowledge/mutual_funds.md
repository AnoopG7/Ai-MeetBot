# Mutual Funds in India

A mutual fund pools money from multiple investors to invest in securities like stocks, bonds, and other assets. Each investor owns units representing a portion of the fund's holdings. SEBI regulates all mutual funds in India.

## Types of Mutual Funds

### By Asset Class
- **Equity Funds:** Invest primarily in stocks (large cap, mid cap, small cap, multi cap, sectoral)
- **Debt Funds:** Invest in fixed-income instruments (gilt, corporate bond, liquid, money market)
- **Hybrid Funds:** Mix of equity and debt (balanced advantage, aggressive hybrid, conservative hybrid)
- **Solution-Oriented:** Retirement funds, children's funds (5-year lock-in)
- **Index Funds/ETFs:** Passive funds tracking an index (Nifty 50, Sensex)

### By Structure
- **Open-Ended:** Buy/sell units anytime at NAV
- **Close-Ended:** Fixed tenure, can buy only during NFO

### Special Categories
- **ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme):** 3-year lock-in, tax benefit under 80C
- **Fund of Funds (FoF):** Invests in other mutual funds

## Key Metrics
- **NAV (Net Asset Value):** Per-unit market value of fund's assets
- **AUM (Assets Under Management):** Total value of fund's holdings
- **Expense Ratio:** Annual fee charged by fund house (max 2.25% for equity, 2% for debt as per SEBI)
- **Alpha:** Excess return over benchmark (positive alpha = outperformance)
- **Beta:** Volatility relative to market (beta > 1 = more volatile)
- **Sharpe Ratio:** Risk-adjusted return (higher is better)

## How to Choose a Mutual Fund
1. Define financial goal and time horizon
2. Assess risk tolerance (conservative, moderate, aggressive)
3. Check fund track record (5-10 year performance)
4. Compare with benchmark and category peers
5. Check expense ratio and fund manager tenure
6. Look at portfolio concentration and turnover ratio

## Taxation (FY 2025-26)
- **Equity Funds:** LTCG (>1 year) over ₹1.25L taxed at 10% without indexation; STCG taxed at 15%
- **Debt Funds:** LTCG (>3 years) taxed at 20% with indexation; STCG as per income slab
- **Hybrid Funds:** Based on equity exposure (>65% equity = equity taxation)
- **Dividends:** Added to income, taxed as per slab

## Regular vs Direct Plans
- **Regular Plan:** Higher expense ratio (includes distributor commission)
- **Direct Plan:** Lower expense ratio (invest directly with AMC)
- Difference: ~0.5-1% p.a. in expense ratio — significant over long term
